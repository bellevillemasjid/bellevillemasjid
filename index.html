FAITH IN ACTION

Water for Life Project

A community effort to bring water to schools in Pakistan through the installation of wells.

PROJECT ARCHIVE

The masjid’s published project report describes a goal of installing 100 wells in schools and documents a well at Government Girls Primary School, Haji Muhammad Gull Killi, Pakistan, supported by the generosity of the Belleville Muslim community.

This is a historical project report, not a current fundraising or completion update. Please contact the masjid for the latest project status and ways to help.

Visiting us for the first time

Everyone is welcome. Please wear modest clothing and remove your shoes before entering the prayer area. Find us at the corner of Moira Street West and Coleman Street, next to Enterprise. Respect marked parking spaces and our neighbours; after busy prayers, consider turning right when leaving the parking lot.
